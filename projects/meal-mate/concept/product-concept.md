# Meal Mate — Concept

Meal Mate is an AI-powered food companion. It helps buy smarter, cook better, and make the most of
what's already at home.

Meal Mate keeps an overview of groceries, nutrition, dietary preferences, recipes, and favourites. It
learns what's on hand and turns that into practical suggestions:

- "What can we cook tonight?"
- "What should I buy this week?"
- "What fits my nutritional goals today?"

From planning the shopping list to guiding cooking and helping use up ingredients before they go to
waste, Meal Mate acts like a knowledgeable friend who takes care of the food side of life.

In short: Meal Mate helps decide what to buy, what to cook, and how to get the most out of every
ingredient.
